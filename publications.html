Flash Alert April 2026

IRD Circular: Revised TDS Rates on Contractor Payments — Effective Chaitra 2082

The Inland Revenue Department has issued a circular updating withholding tax obligations for payments to contractors and service providers. Action required before Chaitra 1.

Contractor Payments (1.5%): The concessional TDS rate for registered contractors continues, but IRD registration verification via the online portal is now mandatory before each payment. Failure to verify reverts the applicable rate to 15%.

Immediate Action: Update vendor master records, configure ERP payment rules, and brief your accounts payable team before Chaitra 1, 2082. Contact the firm for implementation assistance.

News February 2026

ICAN Releases Updated Auditing Standards for FY 2082/83

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nepal has released revised Nepal Standards on Auditing (NSA) aligned with the 2022 IAASB handbook, effective for audits of financial statements for periods ending on or after 31 Ashad 2083.

The updated standards include significant revisions to NSA 315 (Identifying and Assessing the Risks of Material Misstatement) and NSA 540 (Auditing Accounting Estimates), placing greater emphasis on auditor judgment and technology risk.

Firms and practitioners should plan CPE sessions to address the changes before the effective date. Sunil Malla & Associates will circulate a detailed technical summary for clients whose audit engagements will be affected.
