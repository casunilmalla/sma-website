Flash Alert April 2026

IRD Circular: Revised TDS Rates on Contractor Payments — Effective Chaitra 2082

The IRD has updated withholding tax obligations for payments to contractors. IRD registration verification via the online portal is now mandatory before each payment.

Contractor Payments (1.5%): The concessional TDS rate continues, but IRD registration verification is now mandatory before each payment. Failure to verify reverts the rate to 15%.

Immediate Action: Update vendor master records, configure ERP payment rules, and brief your accounts payable team before Chaitra 1, 2082.

News February 2026

ICAN Releases Updated Auditing Standards for FY 2082/83

Revised Nepal Standards on Auditing aligned with the 2022 IAASB handbook, effective for audits of financial statements ending on or after 31 Ashad 2083.

The updated standards include significant revisions to NSA 315 (Identifying and Assessing the Risks of Material Misstatement) and NSA 540 (Auditing Accounting Estimates), placing greater emphasis on auditor judgment and technology risk.

Sunil Malla & Associates will circulate a detailed technical summary for clients whose audit engagements will be affected.
