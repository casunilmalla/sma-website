About Me

You Can Rely On

We Sunil Malla & Associates, an emerging Chartered Accountant firm providing financial and advisory services to businesses, SMEs, and individuals across Nepal.

Our practice is built on a commitment to accuracy, confidentiality, and practical guidance. Whether you are a start-up navigating compliance for the first time or an established enterprise seeking strategic financial counsel, We bring the same rigour and dedication to every engagement.

We hold membership with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nepal (ICAN) and stay current with Nepal's evolving tax laws, regulatory frameworks, and international accounting standards.
